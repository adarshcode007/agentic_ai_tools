======================================================================
  PART 4: GATED MODELS & PRODUCTION BEST PRACTICES
======================================================================
1. What are Gated Models?
   * Models like Meta-Llama-3, Mistral, and Google Gemma are open-weights,
     but require users to accept a license agreement on their HF webpage.
   * If you try to download 'meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3-8B' without an authenticated        
     token that has accepted the license, Hugging Face returns HTTP 403 Forbidden.       

2. Enterprise Docker Best Practice:
   * Never run 'huggingface-cli login' interactively inside Docker.
   * Set the environment variable: ENV HF_TOKEN=hf_... inside your Dockerfile/K8s.       
   * Or mount a shared volume to /root/.cache/huggingface/ to reuse cached weights.
