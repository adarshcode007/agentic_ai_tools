  * Q: What does RPO stand for in disaster recovery?
    A: **RPO = Recovery Point Objective**  

In disaster‑recovery planning, RPO defines the maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured as the time interval between the last successful backup (or replication point) and the onset of a disruption. It answers the question: *How far back in time can we afford to restore data without unacceptable impact?*  

Typical expression: “Our RPO is 4 hours,” meaning backups must be taken at least every 4 hours to stay within the tolerated data‑loss window. (Related metric: **RTO** – Recovery Time Objective, the maximum allowable downtime.)

  * Q: What does RTO stand for in disaster recovery?
    A: RTO stands for **Recovery Time Objective**. It defines the maximum acceptable duration between a service disruption and the restoration of that service to an operational state. In disaster recovery planning, RTO guides the design of backup, failover, and restoration processes to ensure systems are back online within the stipulated time frame.
