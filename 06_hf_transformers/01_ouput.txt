💡 Architectural Insight on Attention Masks:
  Why are attention masks mandatory?
  The 1s indicate REAL words; the 0s indicate PAD tokens.
  In the Transformer Attention equation Softmax(Q * K^T / sqrt(d)),
  mask 0 forces the attention score to -Infinity.
  exp(-Inf) = 0.0, which guarantees that padding words do NOT contribute
  to the attention weights of real words!
